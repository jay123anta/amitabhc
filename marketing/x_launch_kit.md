## STRATEGY NOTES

**When to post**
- Launch thread: weekday 7–10pm IST or Sunday evening (Indian dev + film Twitter both online). If KBC is currently airing, post G-type content right at broadcast time.
- Mark the calendar: **October 11 (Amitabh Bachchan's birthday)** is your biggest reach day of the year — prepare a special post; #HappyBirthdayAmitabhBachchan trends every year in India.

**Reach mechanics on X**
- Native images/video get far more distribution than link posts — that's why the hook has no link.
- A 20–30s screen recording of the Pro IDE running the KBC quiz will outperform static images; post it as a follow-up ("watch AmitabhC run"). You can record with any screen recorder; keep it under 45s.
- Pin the launch thread to your profile; put the IDE link in your bio.
- Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours — early replies boost the thread.
- 2–3 hashtags max per post; more looks spammy.

**Who to engage (don't spam-tag)**
- Do NOT tag @SrBachchan in the launch thread — it reads as spam. If the thread gets traction, ONE polite standalone tweet later: "Sir @SrBachchan, humne aapke dialogues se ek programming language bana di 🙏" with card2. He's active on X and engages with fan creativity; a single respectful mention has a real (if small) shot.
- Reply (relevantly, not promotionally) under big KBC/Bachchan fan pages and Indian tech creators; add value first.
- Communities: post in X Communities like "Build in Public" and "India Tech"; beyond X: r/developersIndia, r/esolangs, r/programminghumor, Hacker News "Show HN: AmitabhC – a programming language made of Amitabh Bachchan dialogues", Product Hunt, dev.to write-up.

**Content cadence after launch week**
- 2–3 posts/week: one keyword spotlight (like posts A/B), one runnable snippet (like C/G), one behind-the-scenes ("why unknown statements now throw errors", "how the KHATAM nesting bug got fixed").
- Recycle the thread each time something ships (new version, VS Code extension on marketplace, etc.).
